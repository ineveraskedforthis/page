Art as an emotional rollercoaster

It might be my crazy personal thing which contradicts more "industrial" mindset but sometimes I think that what you have produced is secondary to what kind of experiences you get during execution of artwork and it's better not to focus on the act of drawing or the result of the said act but manage and savour emotions and qualities of your mood you experience when drawing: frustration, expectations, boredom, hope, despair, desire, helplessness, guilt, pleasure.

It's a wild and emotionally intense ride, but artistic pains are a unique and memorable experience and they are the only thing which truly belongs to you after completing the artwork. Anything "physical" is a collaborative effort of the entire humanity: someone has made your paints and the drawing tablet, mined rare metals and designed the architecture of your PC. You are sculpting your own personal experience and the measure of success is the intensity of these experiences, not the final quality of the artwork.

Feeling demotivation is a natural part of it, sometimes I find myself looking for demotivation and frustration and joking how I would do this or that artwork and then drop doing drawing for a while due to intense demotivation until I start craving these emotions once more.
